OUR INNOVATION

Bacterial Cellulose

A Game-Changing Biomaterial

Bacterial Cellulose (BC) is a game-changing biomaterial derived from natural fermentation. It offers superior strength, flexibility and biodegradability.

Unlike traditional materials containing plant cellulose with chemical treatment such as bleaching, bacterial cellulose is free from chemical processing and is compostable.
