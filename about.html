WHO WE ARE

Transforming Materials Through Innovation

At Ecopoly Biotech, we are committed to transforming the world of materials with bacterial cellulose (BC) — a natural, biodegradable and eco-friendly alternative to plastic.

Our mission is to eliminate single-use plastic waste by offering high-performance, biodegradable and eco-friendly alternatives using bacterial cellulose.
